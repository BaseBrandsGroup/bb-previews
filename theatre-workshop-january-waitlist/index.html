Why families choose us

More than a weekly class. A proper theatre training.

Setting the standard in performing arts since 2003.

Acting, singing and dance

A three-hour session every week, with an hour in each discipline, so your child builds all-round confidence.

Real stages, real shows

Full-scale performances at Birmingham Town Hall and the Ruddock Performing Arts Centre, plus festive performances across the city.

Taught by performers

Every teacher is an established performer, so students learn from people who do it for real.

Exams and opportunities

Preparation for LAMDA and ISTD exams, festivals and auditions, and links with a child acting agency for stage and screen castings.

Questions parents ask

Does my child need experience?

Not at all. We welcome complete beginners as well as children who already love performing.

How much does it cost?

The trial is free. After that, the main three-hour Theatre School is £360 per 12-week term (£30 a session), and payment plans are available.

Is joining the waitlist a commitment?

No. It simply means you hear about January spaces before anyone else.
