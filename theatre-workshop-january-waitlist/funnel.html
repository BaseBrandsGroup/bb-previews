Your build checklist

☐ Check that the pixel is firing (Events Manager › Datasets) ☐ Your web developer has WordPress access and a page builder ☐ Form plugin chosen, and where the sign-ups go ☐ Email system for the emails: Growth Engine, or your current email tool ☐ Tag leads by location and class ☐ Lead event on the thank-you page ☐ Pebble trial booking link for each class ☐ Space numbers for each class ☐ Your photos, welcome video and 3 parent quotes
Pebble can't send the automated follow-up emails, which is the gap Growth Engine fills.
